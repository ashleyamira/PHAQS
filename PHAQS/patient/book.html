How the Waitlist Works

Slot recovery information

When a booked slot becomes available after a cancellation, it is offered to the first eligible patient on the waitlist for 10 minutes.

The patient must accept the offer before the appointment is confirmed. If the offer expires or is declined, it is offered to the next eligible patient.
