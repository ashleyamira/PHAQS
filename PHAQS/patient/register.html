Terms and Conditions & Privacy Policy
1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account and using the Pulilan Health Center Appointment & Queue Management System, you agree to follow the rules, policies, and guidelines of the system. This system is intended to help patients book appointments, monitor queue status, and access basic health center services in a more organized way.

2. User Account Responsibility

Users must provide correct and updated information during registration. You are responsible for keeping your account details safe, including your email address and password. Sharing your login credentials with other people is not allowed.

3. Accurate Patient Information

All information entered in the system must be true and accurate. This includes your name, email address, appointment details, and other information required by the health center. Providing false information may affect your appointment or account access.

4. Appointment Booking

Appointments must only be booked for valid health center concerns. Users should choose the correct health center, service, date, and time based on their needs. Fake bookings, duplicate appointments, and misuse of the queue system are not allowed.

5. Queue Number and Schedule

The queue number given by the system is used for appointment organization. Patients are still expected to follow health center instructions and arrive on time. The health center may adjust schedules depending on emergencies, staff availability, or other operational needs.

6. Proper Use of the System

Users must not use the system for harmful, illegal, or abusive activities. The following actions are prohibited:

  • Creating fake accounts or fake appointments.
  • Attempting to access another patient’s account.
  • Submitting false or misleading information.
  • Trying to damage, hack, or disrupt the system.
  • Using offensive or inappropriate information in any system form.
7. Account Suspension

The health center or system administrator may suspend or restrict an account if the user violates these terms, misuses the system, or provides false information.

8. System Availability

The system may not always be available due to maintenance, updates, internet issues, power interruptions, or technical problems. The system administrators will try to keep the service available, but uninterrupted access is not guaranteed.

9. Limitation of Service

This system is for appointment and queue management only. It does not replace medical consultation, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency response. For urgent medical concerns, patients should directly contact the health center or seek immediate medical assistance.

10. Information We Collect

The system may collect basic personal information such as your name, email address, appointment details, queue information, account registration information, and other details needed for health center services.

11. Purpose of Collection

Your information is used for account creation, appointment booking, queue management, patient verification, health center service coordination, and system record keeping.

12. Data Protection

Patient information is treated as confidential. The system is designed to help protect patient records from unauthorized access, misuse, or unnecessary disclosure.

13. Data Sharing

Your personal information will not be shared with unauthorized third parties. Information may only be accessed by authorized health center staff or system administrators for official health center purposes.

14. User Consent

By registering and using the system, you agree that your information may be collected and used for appointment and queue management purposes.

15. Changes to Terms and Privacy Policy

The Pulilan Health Center may update these Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy when needed. Continued use of the system after changes means that you accept the updated terms and policy.

16. User Agreement

By registering and using this system, you confirm that you understand and agree to these Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. You also agree to use the system responsibly and respectfully.
