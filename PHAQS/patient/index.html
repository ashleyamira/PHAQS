    Priority access

    Extra support where it matters.

    Eligible senior citizens, persons with disability, and pregnant patients can receive priority queue handling after their classification is verified. Emergency cases are handled first through the walk-in emergency workflow.

    Senior citizens

    Patients aged 60 and above can receive priority queue handling.

    Persons with disability

    Verified PWD patients can receive priority queue handling.

    Pregnant patients

    Verified pregnant patients can receive priority queue handling.

    Emergency cases

    Emergency walk-ins are handled first and should not wait for an online appointment.

    Keep your profile accurate

    Complete the applicable classification and bring supporting identification when requested.

    Patient help

    Answers before you book.

    Quick guidance for appointments, arrival, queue tracking and priority access.

    Create an account, sign in, choose an RHU and service, then select an available date and time before confirming.

    Availability and any service-specific requirements are confirmed by the selected RHU. Contact the branch through Patient Inquiries if you need clarification.

    Walk-in accommodation depends on the branch schedule, service and current capacity. Booking ahead gives you a confirmed schedule when slots are available.

    Bring a valid ID, your appointment pass or QR code, and any supporting documents required for the selected service or priority classification.

    After QR or manual check-in, open Queue Status to view your live queue number, people ahead and current serving number.

    Open My Appointments to view the actions currently available for your appointment. Changes depend on its date and status.

    Eligible senior citizens, verified PWD patients, and pregnant patients can receive priority handling. Emergency walk-ins are handled first. Keep your profile classification accurate and bring supporting identification.
